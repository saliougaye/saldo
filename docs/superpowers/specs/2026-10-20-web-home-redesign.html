The new Home of the web app

The Home is one column of four blocks. Each block answers one question. A bar at the top replaces the sidebar.

Language Simplified Technical English (ASD-STE100) Changes G2 decisions D1, D4 and D6 Mockup docs/design/redesign/home-final.html Date 2026-10-20
01

Scope

This spec changes the shell and the Home of the web app. The phone app and the marketing site do not change.

The spec 2026-10-11-web-g2-layout.html stays the source for the other pages. On the shell and on the Home, this spec wins.

The decisions have the prefix H, for Home. The G2 decisions keep their names.

DecisionRule
H1A top bar replaces the sidebar at 760px and more. It holds the brand (D2), the four destinations, “Search”, “New transaction” and the profile avatar. This replaces D1.
H2The content is one column of 1040px in the centre of the page. The inner part of the top bar has the same width.
H3A block is a plain card: bg, a 1px border in border and the card radius. A block has no shadow.
H4The Home has four blocks in this order: Summary, Budget, Transactions, Goals. This replaces D4.

H7Transactions shows “Latest” and “Coming up” side by side. “Coming up” shows the nearest date first.

02

Why

The old Home looked generic. Its parts had no clear reason to be where they were.

The review found three problems:

  • The sidebar has four destinations only. It uses a full column of the screen for them.
  • The columns of the head and of the sections had different ratios (7 and 5, then a grid of 3). The blocks looked placed to fill the space.
  • The style had no point of view. It looked like a template.

The work used three rounds of HTML mockups. The table records each direction that the review did not keep, and the reason.

DirectionResult
EditorialNo cards, lines and white space only. Rejected: the spacing was too loose.
LedgerPaper colours, serif figures, tables. Rejected: a new identity that did not fit Saldo.
Glass and metalTranslucent cards, a dark hero, glow and grain. Rejected: it looked generated by AI.
SheetOne surface, no cards. Not kept: the cards gave clearer groups.
Rail and RowsTwo columns with a fixed status column, or one band for each time horizon. Not kept: “Column” read better from top to bottom.
ColumnKept. One column, four blocks, in the order of the questions.
03

The shell

A top bar holds the navigation. The content and the bar have the same edges.

The top bar (H1). It is 56px high and stays at the top when you scroll. Its background is page at 88% with a blur of 12px. A 1px line in border is under it.

From left to right: the Rocky mark (D2) and the name, the four destinations, a space, “Search” with ⌘K, “New transaction” and the avatar. The current destination has a raised background.

The width (H2). The content column is 1040px wide, with 32px of padding on each side. The inner part of the top bar uses the same width. Thus the brand aligns with the left edge of the blocks, and the avatar aligns with the right edge.

Narrow screens. Below 1000px, “Search” goes to the command palette only. Below 760px, the top bar shows the brand and the avatar only, and the tab bar of the phone shows at the bottom. The tab bar does not change.

04

The order

Each block answers one question. The blocks come in the order in which you ask the questions.

The rule (H4). A block is in its position because of its question, not to fill a space. All blocks have the full width of the column. The page reads from the top to the bottom.

The page head is above the blocks: the period stepper, the period name in 20px, and “day 13 of 30” or “closed period”. The gap between blocks is 16px.

The phone uses the same order. Below 760px, each block is one column.

05

Summary

The big figure on the left, the period trend on the right.

The left column (H5). The label “Left to spend”, or “Saved this period” in a closed period. Then the big figure: the whole part in text, the cents and the currency at 45% of the size in textFaint. Then three values: “Income” in positive, “Expenses”, and “Vs usual”.

“Vs usual” uses accent when the period is above the usual, and positive when it is below.

The trend. The chart is 170px high. It shows this period as a line of 1.75px in text, and the usual as a dashed line of 1.25px. Three grid lines are at 25%, 50% and 75%. The axis shows five dates. A legend names the two lines.

Move along the trend. Move the pointer over the chart, or give it the focus and use ← and →. Home and End go to the first and the last day. A vertical line, a dot and a tooltip show the day. Up to today, the tooltip shows “spent by {date}”. After today, it shows “usual by {date}” and the dot is at 40% opacity.

06

Budget

One ring for each budget, in a row. One sentence tells you what to do.

The ring (H6, H12). Use the TargetRing of the web app at 52px, with ringGeometry from @saldo/ui. The category icon is in the centre, in the category colour. The pace tick shows today. The fill is barGrey, or accent over the limit.

The text under the ring. The budget name. Then the big figure: “{amount} left”, or “{amount} over the limit” in accent (D5). Then “{spent} of {limit}” and the state: “on pace” or “{amount} over pace”.

The advice. One sentence shows under the head, for the featured budget only. featuredTarget picks the budget, and budgetAdvice writes the figure. The budget name comes first, in bold, then targets.perDay or targets.overLimitAction. Four sentences, one for each card, said the same thing four times.

Not budgeted. A line at the bottom of the block, after a 1px line: “Not budgeted {amount}” on the left, “Create a budget” on the right. unbudgetedSpend computes the amount.

The head shows “1 over the limit” or “within limits”, and “All budgets” on the right. With more than 4 budgets, the row goes on a second line.

07

Transactions and goals

The past on the left, the future on the right. Goals are rows with a ring.

Transactions (H7). Two columns of the same width, with a gap of 40px. “Latest” is on the left, with 5 rows. “Coming up · by {date}” is on the right, with 5 rows and “{count} coming up · {amount}”.

A row is 44px high. It has the date, the category icon at 16px in the category colour, the title, the category name in textMuted, and the amount. A recurring row adds the repeat icon. A row of “Coming up” uses textMuted for the title and the amount.

“Coming up” shows the nearest date first. Today homeView sorts the rows newest first. Change the sort in homeView, and check that the phone gets the same order.

Closed period (H8). “Coming up” does not show. “Latest” uses both columns, with 10 rows: 5 on the left, 5 on the right. One column of 5 rows on the full width puts the amounts far from the titles.

Goals (H9). Two columns. A row has a TargetRing of 40px, the name, “{saved} of {target}”, the percent and the state (“{amount} behind” or the amount still missing). A reached goal fills the ring in positive.

08

States

The Home has four states. Each state keeps the four blocks in the same order.

Loading. Each block shows its shapes in raised, with no data. The shapes pulse to 50% opacity every 1.6s. With reduced motion, they do not pulse. The page has aria-busy="true" and a hidden status “Loading…”.

Empty. A block with no data shows its empty title, its empty text and one action. “New transaction” is the only filled button, because it is the first step. The chart shows “No data in this period”. “Vs usual” shows a dash.

Closed period. The page head shows “closed period”. The label is “Saved this period”. The trend covers all the days. A ring has no pace tick, and its state is “Period closed at {percent} of the limit.”. The advice does not show. Transactions follows H8.

09

Motion

You open the Home many times a day. Thus the Home does not animate when it opens.

Motion gives feedback only. Use transform and opacity. Show hover effects only on a device that can hover.

ElementMotion
Button, period arrow, tab “+”Scale to 0.97 (0.94 for icon buttons) on press. 140ms, cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1).
Row, ring tile, goal rowraised background on hover. 120ms, ease.
Trend tooltipOpacity in 120ms. The line, the dot and the tooltip follow the day with no delay.
Loading shapesOpacity pulse, 1.6s. None with reduced motion.
Page, blocks, figuresNone. No entrance, no stagger, no counting figure (H11).

11

Open items

Decide these items in the plan, before the code.

  1. Profile and Admin (D10). Their sub-navigation took the place of the sidebar. With no sidebar, give the sub-navigation a column in the page of Profile and of Admin.
  2. The other pages. Transactions, Statistics and Accounts still use the G2 layout. Make sure that they look correct under the top bar. Their redesign is the next spec.
  3. The order of “Coming up”. Change homeView to the nearest date first. Check the phone, which reads the same order.
  4. 10 rows in a closed period. HOME_ROWS is 5. A closed period needs 10. Add the rule to homeView and write a test for it.
  5. The command palette. “Search” with ⌘K needs the palette. If the palette does not exist yet, do not show “Search”. Done on 2026-10-23: the palette exists, and “Search” is in the top bar from 1000px. See 2026-10-23-web-command-palette.html.
