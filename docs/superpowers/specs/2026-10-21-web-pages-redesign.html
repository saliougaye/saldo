Saldo web · Pages · Spec

The other pages of the web app

Transactions, Statistics, Accounts and Profile move to the shell of the new Home. Every detail and every form opens in one centred dialog.

Language Simplified Technical English (ASD-STE100) Changes G2 decisions D3, D7 and D10, Home decision H10 Mockups docs/design/redesign/: transactions, stats, accounts, profile, empty Date 2026-10-21
01

Scope

This spec changes four pages, the dialog and every empty state of the web app. The phone app and the marketing site do not change.

Read 2026-10-20-web-home-redesign.html first. It sets the shell: the top bar, the column of 1040px and the plain cards (H1 to H3). This spec uses that shell on every page.

The decisions have the prefix W, for web pages. On these pages, this spec wins over 2026-10-11-web-g2-layout.html.

DecisionRule
W1A detail or a form opens in a dialog in the centre of the screen, over a scrim. A detail is 440px wide, a form is 480px wide. This replaces the side dialog of D3. D12 and D13 stay.

02

The dialog

A detail of Saldo holds few values. A side panel of the full height left most of its space empty.

The review compared a side sheet, a split view, a centred dialog and a row that opens in place. It kept the centred dialog (W1).

Transactions. You open a transaction to correct it. Thus the row opens the form at once (W3). The form is the same form as “New transaction”, with these changes:

  • The title is “Edit transaction”. The amount field does not take the focus.
  • Tags, note and repeat show as fields when the transaction has them.
  • The footer has “Delete” on the left, then “Cancel” and “Save”.

Delete. “Delete” removes the row and closes the dialog. A toast shows “Deleted ‹title›” with “Undo” for 5 seconds. “Undo” puts the row back.

Focus. When the dialog opens, the focus goes to the dialog. A form for a new item puts the focus on its first field. Tab stays inside the dialog. Esc and a click on the scrim close it. The focus then goes back to the row.

Motion. The dialog opens with scale 0.96 to 1 and opacity, in 200ms, cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1). It closes in 150ms. The sheet of W2 moves up from the bottom in 260ms. With reduced motion, use opacity only.

03

Transactions

The page answers two questions: how the period goes, and where one transaction is.

The page has three parts, from top to bottom:

  1. Summary. One block in three cells: Expenses, Income, Upcoming. Under Expenses, a bar shows the share of income that you spent. A tick on the bar shows the share of the period that has gone.
  2. Tools. The search field, the filters (account, category, tag) and “Expenses only”. When a filter is on, a line shows the count, the filtered expenses and “Reset filters”.
  3. List. One block. “Upcoming” is a closed row at the top. A click opens its rows. Then the days follow, newest first. Each day shows its date and its expenses.

Keys. ↑ ↓ move along the rows. ↵ opens the row. N opens “New transaction”. / puts the focus in the search field.

04

Statistics

The page answers two questions: how this period compares with the others, and where the money went.

The period block. The block shows Expenses as the big figure, then Income, Net and the end of period projection. Under the figures is the chart “Expenses by period”.

The old chart showed income and expenses side by side, with outlines and min–max lines. The review found it hard to read. The new chart shows one value only (W6):

  • One bar for each period: six closed periods, the current period and three forecast periods.
  • A dashed line for “usual”: the median of the six closed periods. A line under the chart gives its value.
  • The amount of each period under its month. Below 760px, the amounts do not show.
  • The current period: the spent part is filled, the projected part is an outline.
  • A forecast period: a light bar, and “~” before its amount. The min–max range shows in the tooltip and in the table of the forecast.

A click on a bar selects its period. ← and → select the previous and the next period. The page head and every block below follow the selection.

Breakdown. The split bar and the share bar of each row use the colour of the category (W7). The first six categories have their own colour. The others share one segment in barGrey. A row shows the share, the change against usual and the amount. A click opens the detail of the category: the amount, the last six periods, the latest transactions and “All transactions”. “Committed share” is a line at the bottom of the block.

Tags. One row for each tag, then the line of transactions with no tag and the note on tags that overlap.

A forecast period. Breakdown and Tags do not show. “Expected recurring transactions” and “Forecast periods” show. “How it’s calculated” is a line at the bottom of “Forecast periods”.

05

Accounts

The page answers three questions: what you own, where it is, and what you set aside.

  1. Net worth. The big figure is the net worth. Under it, the spendable total and its note. On the right, a line of the net worth for the last 8 months, with the change.
  2. To spend. One row for each account: the icon in the colour of the account, the name, a line with the bank, the change in this period and the free money, a small line of the balance, and the balance. A credit card in debt shows the amount “To repay”.
  3. Savings and investments. The same rows, with “not in the spendable total”.
  4. Goals. A block of its own (W9), in two columns: a TargetRing of 40px, the name, “€1,200.00 of €3,000.00 · in Current account”, the percent and the state. The review also tried goals under their account, and did not keep it.
  5. Archived accounts. One row with the count, at the bottom.

Dialogs. The account detail shows the balance or the amount to repay, the line, the income and the expenses of the period, and the recent transactions. A credit card also shows the amount accrued in the cycle and the next charge. The footer has “Archive account” and “Edit”. A card in debt also has “Settle card”.

The goal detail shows a TargetRing of 72px, the saved amount, the sentence of the goal, “Set aside” and “Withdraw”, the values and the movements. The footer has “Close goal” and “Edit”.

06

Profile and Admin

Profile has nine sections. Admin has five. A top bar cannot hold them, so Profile gets a menu inside its own column.

The way in. The avatar in the top bar opens a menu: the name and the email, “Profile” and “Sign out”. The top bar has no Admin item.

The page. The head shows the avatar, the name and the email. A user with the admin role also sees “Administrator”. Under the head, a menu of 220px is on the left, and the section is on the right.

The menu has these groups, in this order:

  • Account: Profile, Passkeys, Preferences.
  • Organise: Categories, Tags, Budgets, Goals. Each item shows its count.
  • Connections and data: Connectors, Data.
  • Admin · ‹instance name›: Accounts, Invites, Instance, Settings, MCP. Only for the admin role (W10).

“Sign out” is the last item, under a line.

Below 760px. The menu is a list of groups, as on the phone. A row opens its section. The section has a back button to the list.

Sections. A section is a title, an optional lead sentence and blocks of rows. A setting with few values uses a segmented control and saves at once. A row of a list opens a dialog (W4).

07

Empty states

An empty state gives the action that fills it. A short text with one button is not sufficient.

The review compared three ways: text and one action, dashed shapes of the content, and the first step. It kept the first step (W11). The table gives the rule for each place.

The Home. A new user sees a block “Getting started” with four steps and the progress. A done step shows a check and a line through its title. The Budget, Transactions and Goals blocks do not show until they have data. Under “Getting started”, a block shows the money in the accounts. The block of H5 replaces it after the first transaction.

Data. Two rules need new functions in @saldo/core, with tests: the budget suggestions (the median of the last 6 months for each category) and the search in other periods.

09

Open items

Decide these items in the plan, before the code.

  1. The Admin routes. Admin moves into Profile. Decide if /admin/* stays as a route with the Profile layout, or moves to /profile/admin/*. Keep the guard of requireWebAdmin on every page and Server Action.
  2. The phone. The empty states and the edit form open from a row are web rules. Decide if the phone follows them in a later phase. The site shows phone screens (spec 0.8a §04): do not change them now.
  3. New functions in core. Budget suggestions, search in other periods and the emergency fund amount need pure functions in @saldo/core with tests (AGENTS.md, «Tests»).
  4. Keys on Statistics. ← and → change the period. Make sure that they do not act when the focus is in a field or in a dialog.
  5. The URL of a dialog. Today ?t=, ?a=, ?g= and ?c= open a panel. Keep them for the centred dialog, so that a link still opens a detail.
